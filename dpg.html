Digital Public Good & SDG Relevance

FireForm is dedicated to advancing the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals. By eliminating administrative friction, we empower emergency services to operate with transparency, agility, and public trust.

Relevant Sustainable Development Goals

🏢

SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Target 16.6: Develop effective, accountable and transparent institutions at all levels.

How FireForm contributes: By unifying and digitizing the reporting structure for firefighters and emergency responders, FireForm builds stronger, more accountable local institutions. Emergency services can seamlessly share critical incident data across county lines, sheriff departments, and EMS, ensuring transparency and highly effective public service administration without repetitive paperwork.

🏙️

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

Target 11.b: Increase the number of cities implementing integrated policies towards disaster resilience.

How FireForm contributes: FireForm directly enhances a community's resilience to disasters by giving first responders hours of their time back. Instead of performing clerical tasks, firefighters can focus on training, disaster mitigation, and emergency response, creating safer and more resilient communities.

💡

SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Target 9.4: Upgrade infrastructure to make systems resource-efficient and environmentally sound.

How FireForm contributes: FireForm modernizes outdated emergency reporting infrastructure. By leveraging open-source AI locally, it serves as an innovative digital infrastructure that makes emergency management resource-efficient while eliminating redundant paper workflows.

Platform Independence & Open Standards

In accordance with the Digital Public Goods Alliance requirements for Open Software and Open AI Systems, FireForm guarantees platform independence. We do not rely on mandatory proprietary dependencies or closed components that would restrict our MIT License.

  • Frontend: React, Electron, Node.js (Declared in package.json).
  • Backend: Python, FastAPI, SQLite (Declared in requirements.txt).
  • AI System (Optional): Ollama running open-weight models like Mistral. All inference runs locally. The AI features are optional and can be disabled in application settings or swapped with any other LLM service.

Dependency Graph (SBOM): All components in our software supply chain are tracked openly. You can view our full dependency graph directly on our GitHub repository.

Mechanism for Extracting Data

Digital public goods must ensure that data and content can be extracted or imported in a non-proprietary format. FireForm embraces this requirement at its architectural foundation:

  • Standard Format (JSON): All data extracted by the LLM is formatted and exported natively as non-proprietary JSON objects, ensuring zero vendor lock-in.
  • Open Storage (SQLite): Metadata and templates are stored locally in standard SQLite database format (fireform.db), universally readable across platforms.
  • Programmatic API Access: Our local FastAPI backend provides complete REST API endpoints for secure programmatic data extraction.

Privacy by Design & Do No Harm

Emergency incident reports frequently contain sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and patient health records. FireForm guarantees that no data ever leaves the local machine.

This local-first architecture eliminates cloud vulnerabilities and simplifies compliance with HIPAA, CCPA, and GDPR.
