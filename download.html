Specifications

Hardware Requirements & Verification

FireForm runs complete LLM and Whisper transcription models locally. Review the minimum specifications below.

⚡
Processor
Multi-Core 64-bit CPU
Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) recommended for sub-second Whisper transcription; Intel/AMD quad-core minimum.
🧠
Memory (RAM)
8 GB min / 16 GB rec
8 GB minimum for quantized Mistral/Qwen models; 16 GB recommended for concurrent multi-form batch fills.
💾
Disk Storage
4 GB Free Space
Covers the Electron app, embedded PyInstaller backend, local SQLite database, and quantized model weights.
🔒
Network
100% Offline Capable
Zero internet or cellular connectivity needed during operation. No data or telemetry ever leaves your device.

First-Time Setup Instructions

1. macOS: Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag the FireForm icon into your Applications folder. On first launch, macOS Gatekeeper may ask for confirmation since the app runs an embedded local server.

2. Windows: Double-click FireForm-Setup-1.1.2.exe and follow the setup wizard. FireForm will configure local paths and shortcuts automatically.

3. Linux: Open your terminal and grant execute permissions:
chmod +x FireForm-1.1.2.AppImage && ./FireForm-1.1.2.AppImage
